<h1>LightStick Controller for EXO</h1>

A sleek and powerful mobile application that allows you to take control of your official EXO lightstick (v3). Synchronize the LED colors with your music or simply set it to your favorite color.


<h2>✨ Features</h2>
Seamless Connection: Easily pair and connect to your EXO lightstick via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

Manual Color Control: Choose any color from a beautiful color wheel or palette and apply it instantly to your lightstick.

Pre-set Effects: Select from a variety of built-in dynamic effects like pulse, rainbow wave, and strobe.

Music Sync Mode: Let the app analyze your device's audio in real-time and make the lightstick pulse and change colors to the rhythm of the music. Perfect for listening to EXO's discography!

Favorite Colors: Save your most-used colors (like EXO's official fan color) for quick access.

Battery Indicator: Keep track of your lightstick's battery level directly from the app.

Simple & Intuitive UI: A clean, user-friendly interface designed for EXO-Ls of all ages.

🚀 Getting Started
Prerequisites
An Android smartphone with Bluetooth 4.0+ (Bluetooth Low Energy support).

An official EXO v3

Ensure your lightstick has fresh batteries!

Installation
Download the latest APK from https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.niresh23.fanlightcontroller

How to Use
Turn On Your Lightstick: Press the button on your EXO lightstick until the LED turns on and it's discoverable.

Connect: Tap the "Scan" or "Connect" button. Select your lightstick from the list of available devices.

Take Control: Once connected, use the tabs to switch between Color and Music Sync modes. Enjoy!

<h2>🔧 Technical Details</h2>
This app connects to the official EXO lightstick using its public BLE GATT service and characteristic UUIDs to send color and control commands. The app does not modify the lightstick's firmware and operates within the manufacturer's intended parameters.
